# Folk hut atlas glyph

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. This replaces the temporary vanilla
star markers with a distinct, equally simple white hut silhouette.

Source: `assets/icon_sources/omni_folk_hut_glyph.png`.
Runtime: `gfx/interface/map/omni_folk_hut_glyph.dds`, a 64-pixel RGBA texture,
displayed at 20 pixels nearby and 15 pixels at overview zoom.

Prompt:

> Create ONE extremely simple map glyph, a WHITE HUT SILHOUETTE on a genuinely
> transparent background, for an antique atlas UI where cities are marked with
> tiny plain white five-point stars. This must have exactly the same visual
> simplicity as a star or triangle, NOT a drawing of an actual building.
> Front-facing geometric hut: one broad solid white triangular roof, a short
> solid white rectangular body below, one centered doorway cut out as transparent
> negative space. A very tiny white chimney is optional only if the silhouette
> stays instantly readable. Flat pure white fill only; transparent holes/background.
> No black outlines, no gray shading except edge antialiasing, no color, no details,
> no hatching, no thatch, no bricks, no perspective, no texture, no shadows, no
> badge, no star, no extra symbols, no text. Think a minimalist campsite/cartographic
> hut pictogram, readable at just 12x12 pixels alongside plain atlas stars.
> Single compact centered symbol filling about 85 percent of a square canvas.
> Production icon asset, not a mockup or illustration.

Only DDS format conversion and resizing are performed after generation.
