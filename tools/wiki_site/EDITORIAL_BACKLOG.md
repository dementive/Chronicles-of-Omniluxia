# Omniluxia wiki expansion map

This is the curated companion to the generated `wiki-editorial-backlog.md` CI
artifact. The generated report finds thin pages; this document records where the
mod already contains enough canon to expand them responsibly.

## Ready to write from existing canon

These pages have substantial material elsewhere in the mod and can be expanded
without making new setting decisions.

1. **Order of Sennmoggen** — currently 58 words. Mission events and localization
   establish Olltar Sennmogg, Srrovikkabi, the Silver Caves, recruitment
   academies, isolationist oaths, Grand Master Gerrheltt, relations with Hefdarr,
   and the order's possible political intervention. Primary sources:
   `events/mission_events/me_duggmannrum.txt`,
   `localization/english/Omniluxia/01_duggmannrum_l_english.yml`, and
   `localization/english/Omniluxia/choo_heritages_l_english.yml`.
2. **Sumun / Sumun the Great** — the two very short pages should probably be
   consolidated or clearly distinguished. Deity, calendar, invention, and core
   localization describe the Eagelian unification, the Luxterran Calendar,
   Wisheamu and Mina, and Sumun's later veneration. Primary sources:
   `common/deities/00_zanism.txt`,
   `localization/english/deities/deities_zanism_l_english.yml`,
   `localization/english/inventions_l_english.yml`, and
   `localization/english/core_l_english.yml`.
3. **Anempanso** — currently 50 words. Formation and cultural localization cover
   its Copper Dwarven homeland, Frei and Dwadais, the Skyreach Passages, collapse,
   Goblin and Zaraken pressure, and the restoration claim. Primary sources:
   `localization/english/nation_formation_l_english.yml`,
   `localization/english/traits_royalblood_l_english.yml`, and Copper Dwarf
   culture/tradition files.
4. **High Elven Civil War** — currently 94 words. Mission trees, announcements,
   heritage text, lore-start material, and related event localization contain
   its dynastic cause, rival realms, the Great Collapse truce, colonial effects,
   divided navy, and prospective renewal. Primary sources:
   `events/mission_events/me_cymeva.txt`,
   `localization/english/Omniluxia/choo_cymeva_l_english.yml`, and
   `localization/english/Omniluxia/choo_lorestart_l_english.yml`.
5. **Great Northern War** — currently 85 words. Military invention descriptions,
   Timeline entries, country history, and military setup can support an account
   of its belligerents, reforms, campaigns, and consequences.
6. **Imperial Zanic** — currently 51 words. Culture definitions, Zani country
   history, laws, names, religion, and the long-form Zani material can support
   language, administration, class, and post-imperial legacy sections.
7. **Eldritch Forests** — currently 72 words. Map regions, province setup,
   religions, races, and Great Collapse material can establish their geography,
   mana ecology, inhabitants, and weakening after 670 LC.

## Ready after light reconciliation

These have usable sources, but terminology or duplicated pages should be settled
while drafting.

- **Magic Styles / Divine Wells / Melodian / Amten / Aldic** — reconcile the
  short overview pages with `Magic.md`, spell definitions, traits, and magic
  events. Decide whether each is a standalone discipline or a section of a
  larger article before expanding all of them.
- **Ava, Dwadais, Frei, Ferja, Htgarth, and Summsir** — the founding dwarven
  figures appear across racial traits, deities, treasures, Timeline entries, and
  national traditions. Establish a shared template so the paired founders do not
  repeat the same origin story six times.
- **Dragons, Old Dragons, Old Dragkhans, and Mega-Erpons** — the Timeline contains
  a strong evolutionary and mythic sequence, but the taxonomy and naming should
  be reconciled across these four pages before prose is expanded.
- **Waal, Jåam, and Imperial Zanic** — these language pages need examples and
  relationships to their speaker cultures. Existing names and localization can
  provide evidence, but grammar should not be invented from isolated words.

## Needs a lore decision before substantial writing

- **Sea Serpents** is the only article containing an explicit admission that the
  surviving wiki lacks a complete account. Existing canon establishes descent
  from World Serpents, kinship with Snakefolk, and the Serpentine Seas, but not a
  society, government, lifecycle, or modern political role. Those sections need
  authorial decisions rather than extrapolation.
- **Timeline age names marked `CHANGE?`** require canonical names before the
  Timeline and Important Historical Events pages can be cleaned up.
- **Very short primordial or mythic figures** should not be padded independently
  when the setting only supports a single fact. Some may work better as sections
  of a pantheon, myth-cycle, or founding-pairs article with redirects from their
  current page names.

1. Order of Sennmoggen
2. Sumun and Sumun the Great reconciliation
3. High Elven Civil War
4. Anempanso
5. Great Northern War
6. Imperial Zanic
7. Eldritch Forests

10. Sea Serpents, after lore decisions

Each draft should cite its source files in working notes, distinguish direct
canon from synthesis, and add inline links to every existing relevant article.
