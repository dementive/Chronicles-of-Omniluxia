########################################
# CHARTERS - the three terminal endings
#
# BALANCE RULE: a country holds exactly ONE Charter at a time. Choosing a
# different one at the end of a later expedition dissolves the previous one
# (see generic_trade_expedition_revoke_charter_effect). This is what keeps a
# repeatable mission from stacking into an empire-breaking economy.
#
# Each Charter is worth roughly one very good permanent province improvement
# on its primary axis, and each carries a permanent drawback in a different
# currency: the Company costs internal politics, the Monopoly costs
# legitimacy, the Colony costs diplomacy.
########################################
